## Hosted TradingView widget

runtime. This widget and its market data are a separate service, outside the
project's MIT grant. Keep its branding and attribution intact.

Source and terms: [TradingView widgets](https://www.tradingview.com/widget-docs/),

Source: [Bybit API documentation](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/intro).
